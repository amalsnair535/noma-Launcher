# Google Play Store Listing Metadata & Metadata Policy Resolution Guide

This document contains **policy-compliant Google Play Store metadata** designed to fix the Google Play Console rejection notice for **Metadata Policy Violation (Lack of clarity / Inaccurate Description)**.

---

## 🚨 Issue Summary & Policy Explanation

- **Enforcement Location**: Short Description (en-US)
- **Flagged Evidence**: `"No More Attention"`
- **Why It Failed**: Google Play's **Metadata Policy** prohibits vague slogans, marketing taglines, or buzzwords as store metadata. The short description must clearly, accurately, and directly state what the app is (an Android Home Screen Launcher) and its primary purpose.

## 🛠 Step-by-Step Instructions to Resolve Play Console Issue

Follow these exact steps in **Google Play Console** to update your short description and clear the policy flag:

1. Log in to the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Select your app (**FREE Launcher** / **.noma Launcher**).
3. In the left navigation menu, go to **Grow** > **Store presence** > **Main store listing**.
4. Scroll down to the **Short description** field.
5. Replace `"No More Attention"` with the compliant short description:
   ```text
   Minimalist Android home launcher for digital wellbeing, focus, and reduced screen time.
   ```
6. Verify that the **Full description** and **Title** are accurate and policy-compliant.
7. Click **Save** at the bottom right of the page.
8. Go to **Overview** / **Publishing overview** and click **Submit for review** (or **Submit changes**).
9. If an enforcement warning or appeal option is visible in your Policy Center (**Policy > Policy status**), click **Submit Appeal** or confirm that the listing update has been submitted for review. Reviews typically complete within 24–48 hours.
